Help the cat eat every treat in the room. When the last one is gone, the mouse wakes up, and the cat runs over to say hi. The arrows walk and climb the cat posts. Space or Z jumps, and once the cat has a ball of yarn, its tail spins like a helicopter and it flies until the yarn runs out. X or Enter swipes a box out of the way. Pounce on a button to turn off the sprinkler of the same color, and press Down inside a tunnel to come out of the other one. Watch out for the dog, the robot vacuum, the bouncy balls and the cactus pots. A bone makes the dogs sit for a while, and a bubble keeps the cat safe. Esc starts the room over and costs a life, P pauses and M turns the sound off.
